# CASEFLOW v1.1 — Intelligent Agency Operations

This release adds the full agency-intelligence wave requested after v1.0.

## Secure Send
- Operators can send secure portal packages by SMS, email, or both.
- Sensitive documents are never embedded in ordinary SMS/email; notifications contain generic expiring secure-portal links.
- Per-package controls: expiry, revoke, OTP/identity challenge, view/download permissions, selected documents, recipient, and audit events.
- Smart Delivery supports simultaneous delivery or SMS-first with email fallback when unopened.

## Agency Intelligence
1. Permission-aware AI Operations Copilot with audited proposed actions and human confirmation for consequential mutations.
2. Morning Command Brief with KPI summary and prioritized Fix These First queue.
3. Live Agency War Room for real-time intake, contact, qualification, documents, firm capacity, SLA and billing telemetry.
4. Lead Journey Replay built from immutable audit/workflow events.
5. Revenue Intelligence funnel by source/publisher/campaign through submitted, contacted, qualified, docs complete, firm delivered, accepted and billable.
6. Publisher anomaly detection for duplicate, consent, contactability, acceptance and submission-pattern changes.
7. Operational Lead Priority Score; explicitly not legal merit or case-value prediction.
8. Routing Simulator that evaluates proposed routing rules against historical matters without mutating production routing.
9. Agency Command Center geographic heat-map API for state/county volume, qualification, firm coverage and capacity gaps.
10. No-code IF/THEN automation builder with versioned rules, dry-run mode, approvals, audit and safe action allowlists.
11. CASEFLOW Control Tower that aggregates operational findings and provides Investigate / Assign / Resolve / Ignore workflows.

## Safety / governance
- External AI execution remains fail-closed until an approved provider/data-handling policy exists.
- Copilot context is permission-scoped and audited.
- AI may prioritize operations but must not present legal-merit, settlement-value, liability, or legal-advice predictions.
- Automation rules use an allowlist of triggers/actions and human approval for consequential actions.
- All secure-document access remains inside the authenticated/expiring portal layer.
